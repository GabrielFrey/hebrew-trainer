Title: Biti Hebrew
Short description: Modern Hebrew from the first letter to everyday Israeli life.

Full description:
Biti Hebrew is a Modern Israeli Hebrew course with short lessons, a visual learning path, and a checkpoint at the end of every stage.

• Hebrew–English course for English speakers, plus other language pairs
• Deterministic server grading — the tutor never awards XP
• Spaced repetition on a 1 / 3 / 7 / 14 / 30 day ladder
• Stage gates you can pass to unlock the next ulpan level
• Honest unavailable states when audio or speech is not configured

The English product name is Biti Hebrew. Native iOS and Android labels use that name in the English locale.
