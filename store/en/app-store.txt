Name: Biti Hebrew
Subtitle: Modern Hebrew, from Alef to real life
Keywords: Hebrew,Modern Hebrew,learn Hebrew,ulpan,Israel,alphabet,nikud,aliyah

Description:
Biti Hebrew teaches Modern Israeli Hebrew in short, server-graded lessons. Follow Biti along a winding path from the first letters through everyday speech.

You start with the alphabet, first words, and tiny phrases, then move through ulpan stages (Pre-Aleph, Aleph, Bet, Gimel, Dalet, Hey). Each stage ends with a checkpoint you can also use to test out.

Lessons mix typing in Hebrew, multiple choice, and matching. The grader is deterministic. The optional AI tutor explains mistakes and never awards XP.

Audio and speaking practice appear only when a provider is configured. Until then, those items stay honestly unavailable instead of faking scores.

Biti Hebrew is the English name of the product. Other interface languages keep their existing names.
